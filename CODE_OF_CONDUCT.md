# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in RepTracking a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other people
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation
that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
